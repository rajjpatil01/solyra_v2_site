Shipping Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

This Shipping Policy explains how Solyra Lifesciences handles shipping and delivery for products purchased through our official website or other approved channels.

Shipping Availability

Shipping availability may depend on the delivery location, product availability, and courier service coverage.

Order Processing

Orders are generally processed after order confirmation and payment verification. Processing timelines may vary during holidays, high-demand periods, or operational delays.

Estimated Delivery Time

Estimated delivery timelines may vary based on location and courier service. Standard delivery may typically take 3–7 business days after dispatch, depending on serviceability.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed at the time of order placement or communicated before order confirmation.

Delays

Solyra Lifesciences is not responsible for delays caused by courier partners, natural events, local restrictions, incorrect address details, or circumstances beyond our control.

Incorrect Address

Customers are responsible for providing accurate delivery details. Orders may be delayed or undelivered if incorrect or incomplete address information is provided.

Damaged Package

If you receive a visibly damaged package, please contact us as soon as possible with photos of the package and product.
